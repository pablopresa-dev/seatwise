## Direction contract

THESIS: Seatwise is a kit of parts hung on a building that already exists. Like the 1984 Los Angeles environmental graphics (Sussman/Prejza), which dressed the city's existing venues in hot color, stripes and wayfinding instead of building new ones, Seatwise dresses the AI chat the student already uses. Refuses: centered SaaS hero, feature-card grid, purple gradient.

OWN-WORLD: Flat hot fields with no gradients: magenta, vermilion, chrome yellow, aqua, pastel pink and sky, on a cool stucco white; deep plum-ink type. Striped "column" bands, geometric confetti markers (circle, triangle, square, half-disc) as category keys, wayfinding signs with big condensed letters. Barlow Condensed (California public signage lineage) for display, Barlow for text.

STORY: The visitor sees a real-looking conflict-free week, learns it came from their own AI plus Seatwise data, sees restrictions and D-clearance caught in a chat, trusts the privacy rules, and copies the URL.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Drenched magenta field. Striped column at the left edge. Huge condensed headline top-left. A chrome-yellow sign holding the connector URL and copy button directly under it. A full Spring 2027 week grid drawn on the field, bleeding right, Friday column striped "off", 8am row empty.
